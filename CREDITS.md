# RK WORLD — Credits & Attribution

## Project Overview
**RK WORLD** is a standalone 3D browser game created by **Rohith Kumar**.

## Foundation & Game Engine
The core 3D interactive driving experience, physics engine integration, 3D environment, vehicle mechanics, and rendering pipeline are built upon **Folio 2025** created by **Bruno Simon** ([GitHub repository](https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025)), licensed under the **MIT License**.

- **Original Creator & Foundation:** Bruno Simon ([Website](https://bruno-simon.com), [GitHub](https://github.com/brunosimon), [X](https://x.com/bruno_simon))
- **Personalization, Game Identity & Modifications:** Rohith Kumar

## Third-Party Libraries & Technologies
- **Three.js & TSL**: [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) by mr.doob & Sunag (WebGL & WebGPU / TSL rendering)
- **Physics**: [Rapier 3D](https://rapier.rs/) by Dimforge (WebAssembly physics engine)
- **Audio Engine**: [Howler.js](https://howlerjs.com/) by James Simpson / Goldfire Studios
- **Animation**: [GSAP](https://greensock.com/gsap/) by GreenSock
- **Bundler**: [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Camera Controls**: [camera-controls](https://github.com/yomotsu/camera-controls) by yomotsu
- **Debug UI**: [Tweakpane](https://cocopon.github.io/tweakpane/) by cocopon
